# Milestone 1 Briefing

## Context
Milestone 1 is responsible for setting up the solid foundation of ELEPAY Core:
1. Toolchain & build environment alignment (Java, Maven, PostgreSQL).
2. Implementing and hardening algorithms: 001-007, 010, 037-039, 050, 073-074, 080, 085, 088, 095.
3. Completing the end-to-end 12-step internal wallet transfer flow.
4. Ensuring that every financial invariant is enforced: integer minor units, balanced journal entries per currency, ordered row locking, idempotency with SHA-256 fingerprinting, tamper-evident audit logs, rate limits, and zero ledger drift.
